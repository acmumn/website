## Elections!!

ACM officer election season is NOW!

As the first step, we have a period where candidates can declare their
candidacy. This can be done at the form below. However, it should be noted:

- In order to declare candidacy, you must be a current ACM UMN member
- You need two nominations from other current ACM UMN members to support your candidacy
- Nomination form will be open until Friday at 5PM. After that, we'll take all proper declarations and everyone will be able to vote!
- There are more details within the form itself, so if you have questions, make sure to read the form first. Thanks so much!

[acm.umn.edu/nominations](https://acm.umn.edu/nominations)

Positions are very flexible. Knowing about computers helps, but we also need people that can make cool posters or like making friends with potential members. Talk to us, and we can help find a way to get you involved!
