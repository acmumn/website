## Registration 👩‍🏫

Registration is opening up for people this week, so we here at ACM feel an
obligation to let our fellow computer science students in on our favorite
ACM-core classes that are being offered this fall.

Classes required in the CSCI department are NOT included, so these are all
courses picked from love. This is a list in no particular order.

- CSCI 4011: Automata Theory
- MATH 4707: Combinatorics and Graph Theory
- MATH 4281: Intro to Modern Algebra
- ENGL 1181W: Intro to Shakespeare
- CNRC 3201: World of the Hebrew Bible
- PHIL 3305: Medical Ethics
- PHIL 3009: Existentialism
- ARTS 1103: Intro to Printmaking
- OJIB 1101: Beginning Ojibwe I
- ESCI 1006: Oceanography
- LAT 1001: Beginning Latin I
- PE 1048: Honors Bowling

Do with this info what you will, but consider taking an extra class or two for
fun each year. It's a lot easier doing interesting classes as an undergrad than
at any other point in your life.
