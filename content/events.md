# Events

In addition to our weekly open house, we host regular workshops and social events.
Events are open to anyone, and being a member or student is not required.

Open house is every Monday, 4:30pm - 6:30pm, and Friday, 4:30pm - 6:30pm, in our club room, Keller 2-204.

## Officer Meetings

Officer meetings are Wednesdays, 5pm - 6pm.
Feel free to take a look at out past [meeting minutes](@/meeting-minutes/_index.md).

You can also view the meeting topics for the next officer meeting [here](@/next.md).
